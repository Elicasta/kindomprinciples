# Kingdom Principles

## Lesson 1

**The Principle of Identity**

Key thought: **Identity must be settled before pressure comes.**

Primary text: Matthew 3:13-17 through Matthew 4:1-11.

Kingdom principle: **You cannot consistently make Kingdom decisions while allowing pressure, appetite, and people to define who you are.**

The live runtime source for the exact 22-slide deck, seven polls, KJV scripture bank, presenter notes, workbook questionnaire, and visual theme is `/kingdom-principles.js`.

## Series sequence

| Week | Principle | Governing question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identity | What voice is defining me? |
| 2 | Priority | What is organizing my life? |
| 3 | Alignment | Am I living under the authority I want to carry? |
